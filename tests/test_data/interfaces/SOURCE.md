# Protein-interface regression structures

The compressed NextGen mmCIF files in this directory were copied from the
sibling Pinder test fixtures. They originate from the RCSB PDB archive and
cover monomers, homo- and heteromeric interfaces, label/auth chain-ID
differences, symmetry-related interfaces, and biological assemblies ranging
from selectively expanded chains to hundreds of chain instances.

- Pinder fixture source: https://github.com/pinder-org/pinder/tree/main/tests/test_data/pinder_data/nextgen_rcsb
- Structure source: https://www.rcsb.org/
- RCSB PDB data usage: https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
